# Contributing to pikaia

Thank you for your interest in contributing!

The full contributor guide — including codebase walkthrough, step-by-step instructions for adding new strategies, the D-matrix fast path, and the pre-PR checklist — is in the documentation:

**[danube-ai.github.io/pikaia/contributing](https://danube-ai.github.io/pikaia/contributing/)**

Please read our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.
